# Appendix - Choosing the Low-Stock Threshold

Companion to the spec in `../file311.md`.

## Why 5?
- The seed catalog (folder1/folder11/file113.csv) sells slowly: the
  proxy log (`folder2/folder21/file213.log`) shows roughly 1-2 orders
  per hour at peak.
- 5 copies covers about two weeks of demand, which is how long a
  supplier delivery takes.
- The report in `folder3/folder32/file321.csv` flagged "Clean Code"
  (stock=5) as *borderline* rather than urgent - intentional: borderline
  titles are re-checked the following week before escalating.

## When to revisit
- If weekly sales triple, halve the delivery lead time assumption.
- The threshold lives in `folder2/folder22/file221.env.example`
  (`LOW_STOCK_THRESHOLD`); changing it requires no code changes.
